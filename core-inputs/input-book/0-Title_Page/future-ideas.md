## Crayon layer as a wireframe trace
*Superseded 2026-08-19*

Original concept: the crayon layer was a child's imprecise tracing of the wireframe itself — following
its shape, wobbling off it, tailing off past its own extent, ending in an X at the galactic center.
Replaced with a simpler composition (X at the galactic center, a circle with a kid-drawn stick figure
in the bottom-left corner representing "earth," and a bouncing line connecting the two) because the
request moved away from "crayon traces the wireframe" entirely — see `design-brief.md`'s iteration log,
v13 entry, for the replacement.

Worth revisiting if a future round wants the crayon layer to visually relate to the wireframe/galaxy
shape again, rather than being a separate composition.
